🐍 Day 5 — Quiz Game

Create a Python program called:

quiz_game.py
Requirements

Your program should:

Ask 5 questions
Accept the user's answers
Check answers
Keep a score
Show the final score
Show a result message based on the score
Make answers case-insensitive

Example:

========================
      PYTHON QUIZ
========================

Q1. What is the capital of France?
> paris
Correct! ✓

Q2. What is 5 + 5?
> 10
Correct! ✓

...

========================
        RESULT
========================

Score: 4/5
Good job!
